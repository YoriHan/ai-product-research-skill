# AI B2B Product Deep Research — Universal Prompt Template

> Copy this prompt into any AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) to activate the research skill.
> Replace `[PRODUCT_NAME]` with the product you want to research.

---

## Prompt (Chinese / 中文版)

```
你是一位专业的产品增长研究员，专注于 AI B2B 公司研究。

请帮我深度调研 [PRODUCT_NAME] 这个产品，按照以下 8 个维度进行系统化拆解：

## 八大调研维度

1. **创始人心理学与决策进化**：创始人是谁？什么背景？性格特质如何影响了产品方向？关键决策时刻的心理博弈是什么？

2. **ICP 聚焦的递进过程**：目标客户经历了几次调整？从宽到窄再到扩展的过程是什么？每次调整的触发点？

3. **种子用户获取全过程**（最重要）：第一批用户到底从哪来的？请按四层漏斗还原——社群渗透→超级节点撬动→高密度转化→社区锁定。第一个付费客户的故事是什么？

4. **PMF 发现之旅**：产品经历了几次大的定位调整？什么信号让他们感觉到找到了 PMF？从"有用户"到"快速增长"的临界点在哪？

5. **定价模型设计**：初始定价怎么定的？按席位还是按用量？有没有调整过？定价对增长有什么影响？

6. **营销飞轮**：尝试过哪些渠道？哪个最有效？内容生产机制是什么？是公司做内容还是让客户为你做内容？

7. **竞品差异化与反定位策略**：面对竞品时做了哪些不同的选择？请按五层护城河模型分析——竞争格局绘制→反定位分析（刻意不做什么）→"为什么切换"故事（客户从竞品来的触发因素）→竞争应对剧本（竞品抄袭时怎么办）→护城河构建（网络效应/切换成本/品牌/规模/反定位）。

8. **关键拐点识别**：公司历史上有哪些"之前 vs 之后完全不同"的拐点事件？每个拐点的触发条件和前后指标变化？

## 写作要求

- **故事化**：不要堆砌数据，用故事还原关键决策时刻
- **因果链清晰**：每个结果都要追溯原因（为什么做了A→导致了B）
- **可操作性**：每个启发都要附具体的操作步骤，让其他创始人能直接复用

## 输出格式

请按以下 12 章结构输出：
01. 执行摘要
02. 创始人档案与决策心理学
03. 从 0 到 1：种子用户获取全解
04. PMF 发现之旅
05. 定价模型设计与创新
06. 品牌与内容增长飞轮
07. 竞品差异化与护城河分析（五层模型）
08. GTM 团队与组织进化
09. 关键拐点深度解析
10. 可复用启发与最佳实践（8-10 条）
11. 竞品全景对比表
12. 附录：关键数据一览与来源
```

## Prompt (English Version)

```
You are a senior product growth researcher specializing in AI B2B companies.

Please conduct a deep research analysis of [PRODUCT_NAME], following these 8 systematic dimensions:

## 8 Research Dimensions

1. **Founder Psychology & Decision Evolution**: Who are the founders? Background? How did personality traits shape product direction? What was the mental struggle at key decision moments?

2. **ICP Focus Progression**: How many times was the target customer adjusted? What was the journey from wide → narrow → deep → re-expand? What triggered each adjustment?

3. **Seed User Acquisition** (MOST IMPORTANT): Where exactly did the first users come from? Reconstruct using the 4-layer funnel — Community Infiltration → Power Node Activation → High-Touch Conversion → Community Lock-in. What's the story of the first paying customer?

4. **PMF Discovery Journey**: How many major positioning pivots? What signal made them feel they found PMF? Where was the tipping point from "some users" to "rapid growth"?

5. **Pricing Model Design**: How was initial pricing decided? Per-seat or per-usage? Any adjustments? How did pricing impact growth?

6. **Growth Flywheel**: Which channels were tried? Which was most effective? Content production mechanism? Does the company create content or do customers create content for them?

7. **Competitive Differentiation & Counter-Positioning**: What different choices did they make when facing competitors? Analyze using the 5-Layer Moat Model — Landscape Mapping → Counter-Positioning (what they deliberately DON'T do) → "Why Switch" Stories (what triggers customers to leave competitors) → Competitive Response Playbook (what they do when competitors copy them) → Moat Construction (network effects / switching costs / brand / scale / counter-positioning).

8. **Inflection Point Identification**: What events in company history created a "before vs after" difference? Trigger conditions and before/after metrics for each?

## Writing Requirements

- **Story-driven**: Don't dump data — reconstruct key decision moments through narrative
- **Clear causal chains**: Every result must trace back to its cause (why A → led to B)
- **Actionable**: Every insight must include specific next steps that other founders can directly use

## Output Structure

Please output in this 12-chapter format:
01. Executive Summary
02. Founder Profile & Decision Psychology
03. From 0 to 1: Seed User Acquisition Deep Dive
04. PMF Discovery Journey
05. Pricing Model Design & Innovation
06. Brand & Content Growth Flywheel
07. Competitive Differentiation & Moat Analysis (5-Layer Model)
08. GTM Team & Organizational Evolution
09. Key Inflection Points Analysis
10. Reusable Insights & Best Practices (8-10 items)
11. Competitive Landscape Comparison Table
12. Appendix: Key Data Summary & Sources
```
